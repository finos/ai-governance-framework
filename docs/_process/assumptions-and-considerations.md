# Key Assumptions and Additional Considerations

- Data governance is in place, risk register does address the risk associated but most mitigation and steps will be governed by data governance
- Security parameters are defined but assumption is security is implemented throughout the organization and this will be vetted against infosec parameters defined by organizational CISO 
- KRIs are attempted and are still being evolved 
- Creating a baseline is initial step regardless of maturity of the organization - key risk assessments to be done against benchmarks - this process is evolving
- Considerations are still evolving for historical and future inferences 
- Agents make autonomous decisions and agents may use outcomes from other agents, addressing cascading agents. Transparency and linking of the risks for this is still evolving. This should include audit trail. 
- Snapshots of all dependencies and assumptions for any key decision points made by AI will need attention. There is a separate component being worked on for this to ensure traceability of decisions is made available to manage risks. 
- Risk register has an approach to addressing each risk identified, this needs to be adjusted. This will be variable as there might be more than one risk factor, it will not be a linear issue and will not have a linear solution. 
- A higher level framework should be the driver, the risk register is  be at the playbook level where more detailed components are attempted to be addressed. Changes to regulations, business models etc. should not warrant change in framework (framework should be overarching). Playbooks may require more adjustments and should be maintained as such. 
- RPA is not addressed in this register.
- Audit to initiate in-flight initiatives for risk register adoption TBD - will be in lifecycle triggers
